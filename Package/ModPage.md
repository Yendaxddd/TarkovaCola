## Tarkova-Cola {.tabset}

### Description

**Tarkova-Cola brings Call of Duty Zombies Perk-a-Colas to Tushonka!** (SPT 4.1.x, single player)

Drink a perk in raid and it stays active until the raid ends. Level each perk up with the XP you earn, complete challenges to unlock augments, and pair them with drawbacks. The build you equip changes what you chase in raid and which playstyle fits you.

**4.0 "The Last Round" is out: the last big update, and the biggest one.** 6 new perks, 6 more Black Ops 7 weapons, Black Ops animations, machines on every map, compatibility with lots of mods and much more. See below.

**What's new in 4.0**

- **6 new perks**: Quick Revive, Mule Kick, Electric Cherry, Death Perception, Tombstone Soda and Double Tap Root Beer, each with its full Research tree, APEX, jingle, can and injector. Sold by Therapist (loyalty level 1, 7 GP Coin), found in raid and in **Der Wunderfizz**. Highlights:
  - **Quick Revive - Revive**: a lethal hit leaves you **Downed** with only a pistol, crawling. Get a kill in 10 s to get back up.
  - **Death Perception - Sixth Sense**: see enemies through walls. **Killer Instinct**: a warning when someone looks at you from behind, and a UAV pulse on every kill.
  - **Tombstone Soda - Offering**: if you die, your pockets and rig come back by mail.
- **Black Ops 7 weapons**: **Peacekeeper Mk1** (AK-12), **Voyak KT-3** (AK-74M), **MXR-17** (SCAR-L), **VST** (MP9), **M10 Breacher** (M590) and **Hawker HX** (AXMC .338), with their own models, **first-person animations and sounds** from Black Ops 7. MK35 ISR and VMP get them too. Sold by Mechanic (level 1). Compatible with Weapon Camo and Stickers.
- **Black Ops animations**: every perk is drunk with the **Black Ops 7 drinking animation**, and all 23 melee weapons use **Black Ops 7 melee animations** (both on by default, F12).
- **Machines on every map**: perk machines, Der Wunderfizz, the Mystery Box, TEDD and the power switch are now on **all maps**, placed automatically next to walls and loot. Place your own with the **machine editor** (press **End** in raid).
- **Wonder weapons**: with Kenner's **Ray Gun**, **Ray Gun Mark II** or **Wunderwaffe DG-2** installed, the Mystery Box can give them (10%).
- **Perks on enemies, for all 15 perks**: Quick Revive enemies can get back up and hunt you (finish them with a headshot), Electric Cherry enemies shock you when they reload, Tombstone enemies drop a live grenade when they die... Their perk icons float over their heads.
- **Perk case**: a 2x2 case (5x4 inside) for perks and APEX cores. Therapist (65,000 ₽) and flea.
- **45 new achievements** (127 in total), and a new challenge-complete sound (the Black Ops 3 rank-up).
- **Classic mode**: the modifier perk now gives 50% of its effect **plus a fixed signature augment**.
- **Compatibility** with SAM (Advanced Movement), Dynamic Maps, SAIN, QuestingBots, K9 Companion, PIT Fireteam, TacticalReload and more. See the **Compatibility** tab.
- **Translations**: every text can be translated with JSON files. See `TRANSLATING.md` in the zip, and send yours to get it bundled.
- **Balance pass**: cheesable challenges fixed, lower goals, and the drawbacks that were generic now hit their own perk (Cold Armor, Own Wave, Tired Eye, Picky Vulture, Fleeting Edge, Noisy Reload).
- **Other**: PHD Flopper no longer lets explosions wear your armor; F12 volume for jingles and effects; F12 *Pause perk effects*; Bandolier and Widow's Wine have their own cans; perks can't be drunk in the Hideout.
- **Removed**: Synergies (their achievements are hidden) and the developer tools in F12.

**Also in the mod**

- **Two modes**, switch them in Research:
  - **Modern**: full progression with augments and drawbacks. Drink a perk you already have and it becomes an **Eternal Perk** (blue outline): it stays with you between raids, loses a drawback and comes back by mail if you die.
  - **Classic**: no Research and no drawbacks. Up to 4 perks plus 1 **modifier** (50% effect + its signature augment), each with a random minor augment. **Your perks stay with you until you die.** Every raid brings an optional challenge that grants a second augment.
- **APEX**: one **very strong** effect per perk (see the APEX tab). One at a time, in Modern and Classic.
- **Perk machines, Der Wunderfizz, the Mystery Box and TEDD** on the map (see the Map tab).
- **Random Perk power-up**: kills can drop a floating bottle (4%, once per raid, 60 s to grab it), with announcer voice.
- **The Bottlers storyline** (needs **VisitAPI**): 10 quests from Skier and Therapist about a crew of ex-PMCs who got their hands on the Perk-a-Colas. The final quest pits you against **the Bartender**, who carries every perk.
- **Crafting**: brew perks in the hideout **Nutrition Unit** (recipes unlocked through the storyline).
- **127 native E∗∗ achievements** (Profile > Achievements), rewards by mail.
- **Perks all over the world**: crates, safes, caches, duffle bags, jackets, loose loot... **Bosses always carry 2 perks**; PMCs, Rogues and Scavs sometimes carry one. Chances are in `user/mods/TarkovaCola/loot.json`.
- **Perks for PIT Fireteam teammates**: 4 perk slots in their inventory; they keep them until they die.
- **Perks as injectors** (optional, F12), **le creatura** grenade (and its Mii version) on the flea market, and **Arena Mode** compatibility.

**How to use**

1. Get a perk:
   - buy it from **Therapist** (loyalty level 1, **7 GP Coin**),
   - buy it from a **perk machine** or **Der Wunderfizz** in raid,
   - find it in raid, win it from **TEDD**, take it from an enemy, or craft it in the hideout.
2. Drink it in raid.
3. Earn XP in raid (+25% while the perk is active) to level it up (max level 5).
4. Open **RESEARCH** in the main menu (bottom bar, next to Handbook, or press **F9**) to see your progress, equip augments and drawbacks, pick an APEX, and switch modes.
5. Press **ESC** in raid to see your level and pending challenges.

**Requirements**

- **SPT 4.1.x**.
- **Optional: VisitAPI 1.3.5+** for the storyline. Without it there are no quests and every perk is sold by Therapist from the start; everything else works the same. **With** it, each of the 6 original perks unlocks its sale at Therapist with its quest (you can still find them, buy them from machines and craft them). The 9 newer perks are always sold. Debug option in F12: *Perks for sale without quests*.

**Installing / updating:** extract the zip over your SPT folder and overwrite files. Your progress, Eternal and Classic perks are kept (also when updating from 1.x, 2.x or 3.x).

**Uninstalling:** delete the `TarkovaCola` folders. **Before that, move the weapon out of the Mule Kick slot** into your stash: without the mod that slot no longer exists and SPT will treat the weapon as a broken item.

### APEX

Every perk has an **APEX**: a single, very powerful effect. You can only run **one** APEX at a time (Modern and Classic), and it only works while its perk is active.

1. Get an **APEX Core**: rarely found in safes, caches, weapon boxes and PMC bodies. Mechanic (level 1) also sells it for 5,000,000 ₽. It is absorbed when you pick it up (or carry it into a raid); **extract alive** to keep it. Once you have no APEX challenges left to start, cores are no longer absorbed and you can sell them.
2. In **Research > APEX**, spend a core to start that perk's APEX challenge. It only progresses while its perk is active.
3. Complete the challenge and equip it.

### Map: Machines, Mystery Box & TEDD

*On every map.* Maps without hand-placed spots get them **automatically**: next to walls, close to loot, spread out.

- F12 > Machines > *Automatic machine placement*: **Fixed** (found once and kept), **Every raid** (new spots every raid) or **Off**.
- F12 > Machines > *Redo automatic machines*: the next raid throws away that map's automatic spots and finds new ones.

**Machine editor** (F12 > Machines, or **End** in raid): place your own pieces anywhere. Hand-placed pieces always win over automatic ones.

| Key | Action |
| --- | ------ |
| PageUp / PageDown | Next / previous piece (perk machines, Der Wunderfizz, Mystery Box spot, TEDD spot, power switch) |
| [ / ] | Rotate |
| \\ | Snap the rotation to 45° |
| Insert | Place |
| Delete | Delete the nearest piece |
| F11 | Screenshot |

**Perk machines**

- Hold **F** in front of a Perk-a-Cola machine to buy its perk for **30,000 ₽**. If you already have it, make it **Eternal** for **15,000 ₽**.
- Machines stay off until you **turn on the power** with the map's power switch (this rule can be turned off in F12).
- **Der Wunderfizz** gives a random perk for **40,000 ₽**, with its roulette. It is the only machine for the 9 perks without their own (Bandolier, Vulture Aid, Melee Macchiato and the 6 new ones).

**Mystery Box**

- Spawns at **one** of its spots each raid. Each spin costs **1,000 ₽**.
- **Hold F** for a **random weapon** (built, with its preset). **Hold Y** (configurable in F12) for a **random attachment**.
- Just like Zombies: the lid opens, the models cycle and the prize floats. Grab it before it sinks back.
- After several uses in the same spot the **teddy b∗∗∗** may show up: you get your money back and the box flies off to another spot.
- 15% chance to give a Black Ops 7 weapon, and 10% chance to give a **wonder weapon** if you have Kenner's Ray Gun, Ray Gun Mark II or Wunderwaffe DG-2 installed.

**TEDD**

- Shows up in **some raids** (15% by default, configurable in F12) at one of his spots. He glows green and is marked on screen when you are close.
- Hold **F** to talk to him and he gives you **one random trial**, with 5 minutes to complete it:
  - **Item:** an item spawns far away; bring it to him.
  - **Takedown:** a PMC spawns, marked on screen; it has to die.
  - **Hold out:** stay near TEDD for 90 s while waves of infected come in.
  - **Money:** pay him the amount he asks for.
- **Reward:** a random perk bottle.

**Perks on enemies and teammates**

Enemies carrying a perk get its effect, and you get a notice when one triggers. Their perk icons float over their heads (in line of sight, within 60 m; F12 > *Perk icons over enemies*).
